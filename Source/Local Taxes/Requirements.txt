A taxpayer arrives at the local tax payment office.

The clerk searches for the taxpayer (by name or personal identification number – CNP), informs them of the total amount they owe for the current year for all their properties, and can then collect either the full amount or a partial payment.

Each taxpayer may own multiple properties: buildings and/or plots of land.

Each property is located at an address (an address consists of a street name and a street number).

The amount owed each year for each type of property is calculated as follows:

- **For a building:**  
  `500 * building area (m²)`

- **For a plot of land:**  
  `350 * land area (m²) / rank of the locality where the land is located`

A locality can have a rank of 1, 2, 3, or 4.

Regardless of whether they have made a payment or not, the taxpayer can request a payment statement containing all the properties they own, together with the amount they owe for the current year for each property.

The statement looks the same regardless of whether the taxpayer has already paid any amount for the current year.

### Payment Statement Example

```text
Taxpayer: Ion Popescu

Properties

Building: V Parvan Street, No. 2
Area: 20
Cost: 10000

Land: V. Parvan Street, No. 2
Area: 10, Rank: 1
Cost: 3500

Building: Lugoj Street, No. 4
Area: 25
Cost: 12500

Total amount: 26000
```

### Requirements

Implement some of the classes identified above so that the following operation can be performed:

After finding the taxpayer Ion Popescu, the clerk displays the corresponding payment statement for that taxpayer.

In the `main` method:

1. Instantiate the class that models the concept of a taxpayer.
2. Set up the properties belonging to that taxpayer.
3. Display the taxpayer's properties and the corresponding payment statement.
