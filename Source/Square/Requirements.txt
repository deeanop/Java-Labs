A square is characterized by its side length.

Write a class called `Patrat` (Square) that has two constructors:

1. A parameterless constructor that sets the side length of the square to `10`.
2. Another constructor that sets the side length to a value equal to the parameter passed to the constructor.

Add an appropriate method to the class for printing a square in the following format:

`"Square" l "Area" a`

where:
- `l` is the value of the square's side length.
- `a` is the value of the square's area.

Create several objects of type `Patrat` inside a `main` method and print them.
